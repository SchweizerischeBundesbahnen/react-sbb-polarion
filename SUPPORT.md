# Support

## How to get help

- **Questions and general help**: open an [issue](../../issues/new/choose) and label it `question`.
- **Bug reports**: open a [bug report](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Feature requests**: open a [feature request](../../issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Security issues**: follow the organisation's
  [security policy](https://github.com/SchweizerischeBundesbahnen/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
  Do not open a public issue for a security vulnerability.

Before opening an issue, please check [`README.md`](./README.md) for how to build, test and consume the
package, and [`CLAUDE.md`](./CLAUDE.md) for the repository's known traps - several of the questions that
reach us are answered there, in particular why a visual test diffs on a developer machine but not in
CI.

## What to expect

The maintainers review issues on a best-effort basis. Response times depend on the team's capacity.

## Supported versions

Fixes are released from `main` as a new version on
[npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@sbb-polarion/react-sbb-polarion). There is no long-term support
branch: please upgrade to the current version before reporting a bug.
